Research Question

Can machine learning methods reproduce and extend the causal findings of Saadaoui (2026)? We apply Double ML and panel local projections to test robustness, extending the original US–China and Japan–China dyads to 11 additional bilateral relationships with China.

Identification Strategy

The instrument is Δ²PRI — the second difference of the bilateral Political Relationship Index, constructed from Chinese government and media sources:

Δ²PRIt = PRIt − 2·PRIt−1 + PRIt−2

Sharp deviations from established diplomatic trajectories are exogenous to oil markets. The US–China first-stage F-statistic exceeds 190, confirming strong identification.

Estimation Methods

  • Linear IV-LP — baseline, following Saadaoui (2026) and Jordà (2005)
  • Double ML (DML-PLIV) — XGBoost nuisance partialling; confirms linear spec adequate (0/49, stable SE)
  • IVW meta-analytic pooling — inverse-variance weighted across dyads; I²<5% for strong dyads
  • CCE-LP (Pesaran 2006) — corrects for cross-sectional dependence across dyads
  • CF panel — control-function estimator; 0/49 due to common-slope restriction

Core Findings

  • Placebo test (500 permutations): p = 0.000 — result is not random
  • Sign reversal confirmed: negative at h=6, positive at h=32
  • GPR-USA reduced form: 41/49 significant horizons
  • GPR-China reduced form: 3/49 — Chinese domestic risk does not drive oil
  • Structural break at 2015: Chow p < 0.001 at every horizon tested
  • Pre-2015: 25/49 significant. Post-2015: 5/49

Honest Limitations

  • Small sample n=385 — limits DML convergence and panel power
  • Instrument strong only for US–China and Japan–China; most other dyads F<15
  • Japan–China: F=113 but 0/49 significant — the channel is US-specific
  • Asymmetry test is directionally consistent but severely underpowered (MDE=1.40, observed diff=0.25)
  • No Python equivalent of Stata's ivqregress; quantile results are exploratory

Data Sources

  • PRI: Tsinghua University Institute of International Relations (bilateral, 1990–2022)
  • WTI: EIA monthly spot price (log real), via Saadaoui (2026) replication dataset
  • Controls: OECD+6 industrial production, global oil production (Baumeister & Hamilton 2019)
  • GPR indices: Caldara & Iacoviello (2022)
  • GDELT: structured event tone (NB03, partial coverage)
  • Sample: January 1990 – February 2022 · n = 385 monthly observations
