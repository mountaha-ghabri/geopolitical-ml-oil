Optional free Chinese news (no API)

1. Clone or download text files from:
   https://github.com/brightmart/nlp_chinese_corpus/tree/master/news2016zh

2. Copy .txt files into this folder (any subfolders OK).

3. Re-run notebook 04_construct_gpr_from_articles.ipynb

For People's Daily history, search Hugging Face for open "People's Daily" datasets
(download once, export to .txt). No paid license required for academic use of open mirrors.
